## Key decisions
- A missing email key secret disables the digest rather than failing the job: the same rule as a missing sender,
  and the job never half-sends.
- Keep the local `PageLayout` as a thin adapter over the theme's: ten pages keep their props, the wrapper markup
  comes from the theme.
- `publicPaths` lists the guard's own public paths, so the Access bypass list and the app's guard cannot drift.
